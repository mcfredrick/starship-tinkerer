# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Ilse: getting slower

== ilse_arrives
* The Heron slides into the bay before the doors are fully open. Ilse climbs out and checks over her shoulder.
ilse grumpy: Close the doors. I don't want the whole dock watching.
ilse thoughtful: The Heron's lost a tenth of a second a lap. Every lap. For a month.
> Could be the coupling. Could be a dozen things. => ship
> Could it be the pilot? => pilot
-- pilot
ilse grumpy: ...
ilse sad: That's what I'm afraid of. Eleven years I've held the club title. Nobody holds anything forever.
ilse thoughtful: So. Find out if it's the ship. If it isn't, I'd rather hear it from you than from the timing board.
! set ilse_confided
=> terms
-- ship
ilse: That's what I'm hoping. A dozen things I can fix. The other thing I can't.
=> terms
-- terms
ilse: Three hundred and forty pebs. And a little more on the straights while you're in there.
ilse grumpy: And not a word to Brindle. It'll want to measure my feelings.
! job ilse_1
end

== ilse_1_delighted
* Ilse takes the Heron round the ring once, alone, and comes back with her visor up and her eyes bright.
ilse surprised: A tenth. No. Two tenths. Under my old record.
ilse laughing: It was the ship! It was the blasted coupling! Eleven years, and I nearly retired over a coupling!
ilse happy: Here. Full price and a bit. And {name}? Thank you. For not saying the other thing.
end

== ilse_1_pleased
ilse happy: She's quicker. Not what she was at her best, but quicker.
ilse thoughtful: Maybe that's as good as either of us gets now. Here's your pebs.
end

== ilse_1_lukewarm
ilse thoughtful: Same tenth. Maybe a little less.
ilse sad: Well. Maybe it is me, after all. Here. You did what you could.
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Odessa: roam or root

== odessa_returns
* A familiar rattle-free hum. The Pocket Moth noses into the bay, and Odessa waves through the window before she's even parked.
odessa happy: {name}! I told you I'd be back through Quietwater. I've been thinking, like I said.
odessa thoughtful: Sixty years I've been flying. Every moon in the system, and a few that aren't on maps. But the grandkids are growing up three moons apart, and I keep arriving just after the important bits.
odessa: So. I could settle somewhere. Near one of them. Or all of them could come to me, and we keep flying, the lot of us, in the summers.
> Put down roots. They'll come to you. => roots
> Keep roaming, and take them along. => roam
-- roots
odessa surprised: Roots! At my age. Well, why not? Quietwater's as good a pot as any.
! set odessa_roots
=> job
-- roam
odessa laughing: Oh, I was hoping you'd say that. I'm not done with the sky yet.
! set odessa_roams
=> job
-- job
odessa: Either way, first things first. All three of them are coming for the Close Pass, and I want them in the Moth with me, noses to a big window.
odessa happy: Two hundred and sixty pebs, and three seed cakes, and I'm not taking no for an answer about the cakes.
! job odessa_2
end

== odessa_2_delighted
* Odessa climbs into the new cabin and sits in each seat in turn, bouncing a little.
odessa laughing: Look at that window! They'll fight over it. I'll let them. It'll be lovely.
odessa happy: I can already hear them. Every word. Oh, {name}. Here, take the cakes before I cry on them.
end

== odessa_2_pleased
odessa happy: There we are! Room for all of us, and a window to squash our noses on.
odessa: Your pebs, and the cakes. Thank you, love.
end

== odessa_2_lukewarm
odessa thoughtful: Well, we'll all fit. Just about. Cosy, we'll call it.
odessa: Here's what we agreed. They'll love it anyway. Children love anything that flies.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Brindle: measuring joy

== brindle_returns
* The Bucket clanks into the bay with a fresh coat of happy yellow. Brindle hops out, antenna wobbling.
brindle happy: {name}! I have news. I have entered the Drum Garden Dash.
brindle thoughtful: It is the twistiest course on the dock. I have measured my joy in twists and it is very high. Higher than corners. Corners are just shy twists.
brindle: I would like the Bucket to stick, and to turn, and to have a gadget. A gadget is a friend you bring.
> What sort of friend? => friend
> I'll make her a twisting champion. => champ
-- friend
brindle happy: Any sort. A magnet friend. A honking friend. A bubble friend. I am not fussy about friends.
=> champ
-- champ
brindle laughing: Hooray! I have one hundred and fifty pebs and two tins of Drift Bloom. I do not drink tea. I like the smell.
! job brindle_2
end

== brindle_2_delighted
* Brindle drives the Bucket in a slow figure eight around the bay, then another, then a third, very slowly.
brindle laughing: It twists! It twists like a happy thought that is also a ribbon!
brindle happy: I am going to win. Or I am going to come second and be just as happy. I have checked, and I would be just as happy.
end

== brindle_2_pleased
brindle happy: It twists better. That is very good. Thank you, {name}.
end

== brindle_2_lukewarm
brindle thoughtful: It twists about the same. That is all right. I will twist with my whole self instead.
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the Close Pass

== close_pass_eve
* The tablet chimes with a dock-wide notice: THE CLOSE PASS IS TOMORROW. The comet will come nearer Quietwater than at any time in fifty years.
* Every shop on the dock is closing for the evening. Every ship that can fly is flying out to watch.
* Somewhere in the back of the hangar, Grommet is already sitting by the bay door.
end

== close_pass_call
* Before you've even put the kettle on, there's a knock at the bay door. Odessa, Brindle, Ilse, and Crake are standing in a row, which is a strange sight.
odessa happy: Come on, love! The shop can spare you for one night. You fixed half the ships going.
ilse: Bring your own ship. Nobody watches a comet from someone else's.
brindle laughing: I have brought snacks. I do not eat snacks. I brought them for joy.
* Grommet walks past all of you and gets into your ship first.
end

== close_pass
* Out past the edge of the rings, the ships of Quietwater hang in a loose circle, lights dimmed, engines purring.
* And then the comet comes. A bright head the size of a moon, and a tail that fills half the sky, silver and pink and slowly unfolding.
? odessa_roots => odessa_roots
odessa happy: Look at them, {name}. Noses on the glass. Every one of them talking at once.
odessa laughing: This summer they're coming roaming with me. All three. The Moth's got room now.
=> ilse
-- odessa_roots
odessa happy: Look at them, {name}. Noses on the glass. Every one of them talking at once.
odessa: I've taken the old flat above the drum gardens. They'll come to me now. I'll be your neighbor, love. Your cake supplier.
=> ilse
-- ilse
? ilse_1_lukewarm => ilse_slow
ilse thoughtful: Garden Dash next. My last race of the season. Maybe my last, full stop. I'd like you on the grid.
ilse happy: Don't go easy on me. I'll know.
=> brindle
-- ilse_slow
ilse sad: I'm racing the Garden Dash. Maybe my last. If it is me who's slowing down, I'd like to find out against someone good.
ilse thoughtful: Will you be on the grid?
=> brindle
-- brindle
brindle surprised: {name}. I have measured my joy.
brindle thoughtful: It is the highest it has ever been. And I am not moving at all. I am sitting still and watching.
brindle happy: I did not know joy could be sitting still. I will have to measure everything again.
? crake_2_delighted => crake_kind
? crake_2_pleased => crake_kind
* The Magnificence hangs a little apart from the rest. You can see an elderly lady at its window, pointing things out to her son.
=> finale
-- crake_kind
* The Magnificence drifts over, close enough to talk. An elderly lady in a feathered hat waves to you from the window.
crake thoughtful: Mother wants to know who made the cabin so quiet. I told her. She says I'm to be nicer to you.
crake happy: I said I'd consider it. Thank you, {name}. Truly.
-- finale
* The comet's tail sweeps over the dock, and for a moment every hull in the circle glitters silver.
* Back home, you'll find a little of that silver stays in your eyes. You could mix a paint like that.
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Lumen Reach

== lumen_call
* Your tablet chimes. It's Themba, the lighthouse keeper: "If you're coming, come tonight. The ice is clear and the whale's close."
* Grommet is sitting in your ship's open hatch, looking at you as if you're the one who's late.
end

== lumen_arrive
* At the edge of the rings, past where the maps get vague, a field of ice drifts in the starlight. In the middle of it stands a lighthouse, striped red and cream, its great lamp dark.
* A small figure waves from the gallery rail. A cat beside them does not wave.
* Themba leads you up the spiral stairs to the lamp room. The gears are humming a low, lost note.
> Put a hand on the housing and listen. => listen
-- listen
* There. Under the hum, two notes that don't agree. Something's drifted out of true.
* The calibration dials are old brass and stiff with cold. You'll have to bring the two notes back together.
end

== lumen_after
* The gears catch, and settle, and hum one sure note. The great lens begins to turn.
* The gold arm sweeps out across the ice, once, twice.
* And out of the ice field rises a whale: long and pale and gentle, spots glowing along its sides, its eye as big as the lamp. It sings, long and low, and the glass of the lamp room sings back.
* Themba is crying a little, and laughing. The cat sits down on the logbook.
* Before you leave, Themba presses something into your hand: a shard of old beacon glass that still holds a little of the gold light.
end
