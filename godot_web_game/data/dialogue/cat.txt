# Grommet, the shop cat Halloran left behind. A moody tuxedo with a green collar and a gold tag.
# One scene plays some mornings (see CatMood); "! cat <effect>" does the helping or the mischief.

== grommet_intro
* Something shifts on top of the parts shelf. Two green eyes open in the gloom.
* A black-and-white cat drops to the floor, lands without a sound, and inspects you from your boots upward.
grommet thoughtful: Mrrp.
* The gold tag on its green collar reads GROMMET. On the back, in Halloran's handwriting: "Came with the shop. Will not be leaving."
> Hello, Grommet. => hello
> Are you allowed in here? => allowed
-- hello
grommet happy: Mrrrow.
* Grommet bumps your shin once, as if stamping a form, and strolls off to sit in the warmest patch of the hangar.
end
-- allowed
grommet grumpy: Mrr.
* Grommet looks at you the way you'd look at someone asking if the floor is allowed in here.
end

== cat_sniff
* Grommet is standing very still in the middle of the bay, nose working.
grommet thoughtful: Mrrp?
* They pad over to the cradle, sniff along a seam, and sit down pointedly beside one spot.
* Huh. There's something there.
! cat sniff
end

== cat_gift
* There's something on your workbench that wasn't there last night. Grommet is sitting next to it, looking extremely pleased.
grommet happy: Mrrrow!
* It's a part. Slightly chewed. Where Grommet got it, you'd rather not ask.
! cat gift
end

== cat_cuddle
* Grommet follows you everywhere this morning, chirping, and keeps glancing toward the tea nook.
grommet happy: Prrrp.
* It's very clearly a hint.
! cat cuddle
end

== cat_hide_part
* One of your shelves has a suspicious gap in it.
* Grommet is washing a paw on top of the drawers and not making eye contact.
grommet thoughtful: ...
! cat hide_part
end

== cat_return_part
* The missing part is back on your shelf this morning, set down precisely in its old spot.
grommet happy: Mrrp.
* Grommet acts as though this is a great kindness on their part.
! cat return_part
end

== cat_bench
* Grommet has claimed the workbench. All of it. They're lying across the tools like a small black-and-white rug.
grommet grumpy: Mrrrrr.
* You'll be working around them today. Carefully.
! cat bench
end

== cat_spill
* A crash from the tea nook. A tin rolls slowly across the floor, trailing tea leaves.
grommet surprised: !
* Grommet stares at the tin as though it attacked first, then leaves at speed.
! cat spill
end
