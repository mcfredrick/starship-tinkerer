# Day three: Thaddeus Crake and the Magnificence. A haggler, and the first real temptation.

== day3_morning
* A chime sounds before the shop doors are even open. Then another. Then four in a row.
end

== crake_arrives
* A long plum-and-brass yacht slides into the bay, takes up both bays, and stops with its nose six inches from your tea nook.
crake grumpy: You. The new one. Halloran's replacement.
crake thoughtful: I suppose you'll have to do. I am Thaddeus Crake. This is the Magnificence. You may admire her.
> She's very... plum. => plum
> Good morning to you too. => morning
-- plum
crake happy: Isn't she? Plum is the color of people who matter.
=> job
-- morning
crake grumpy: Is it? I hadn't noticed. I've been waiting outside for nine minutes.
=> job
-- job
crake: I want her to be the fastest yacht on the ring, and I want her to shine like money. Not that you'd know what that looks like.
crake thoughtful: The posted rate for this sort of thing is two hundred and eighty pebs. I'll give you one sixty.
> Two eighty, or find another shop. => firm
> One sixty is fine. => cave
-- firm
crake grumpy: Hmph. Halloran never haggled. Fine. Two eighty. But I'll be watching.
! job crake_1
! set crake_held_firm
end
-- cave
crake happy: Splendid. I knew you were sensible. Sensible people are so easy to work with.
* He strolls out without a thank-you. The yacht's horn plays four notes of a march as he goes.
! job crake_1
! budget 160
! set crake_haggled
end

== crake_1_delighted
* Crake circles the Magnificence, cane tapping, saying nothing for a long time.
crake surprised: Well. Well, well. She's... yes. She's exactly right.
crake thoughtful: Don't let it go to your head. I'll mention your shop at the yacht club. Quietly.
* He counts out the pebs with great ceremony and, after a visible struggle, adds a tip.
end

== crake_1_pleased
crake thoughtful: Adequate. Better than adequate, if I'm generous, and I'm rarely generous.
crake: Here. Payment, as agreed. Not a peb more.
end

== crake_1_lukewarm
crake grumpy: This is it? This is what Quietwater calls craftsmanship?
crake: I'll pay, because a Crake pays his debts. But I'll be telling people.
end

== crake_1_prank
* Crake settles into the pilot's seat and taps the horn to announce his departure.
* It honks. Or squeaks. Or something is grinning at him from the dash. The bay goes very quiet.
crake surprised: What. Is. That.
> A complimentary upgrade. => straight
> I have no idea what you mean. => straight
-- straight
crake grumpy: You think you're very funny.
crake thoughtful: ...
crake grumpy: It is, I'll admit, slightly funny. Do not tell anyone I said that.
* He leaves anyway. You get the feeling this isn't over.
end

== crake_1_sabotage
* The Magnificence lifts off, wobbles, and something goes "pff."
crake surprised: What was that? What did you DO?
crake grumpy: You'll be hearing from my solicitor. I have three. They all hate you already.
* He pays. Grudgingly, and in the smallest coins he has.
end

== day4_morning
* Morning on Quietwater, and the dock has settled into its rhythm with you in it.
* Word of the new tinkerer has got around. There are requests pinned to the job board, from folk you've never met.
end
