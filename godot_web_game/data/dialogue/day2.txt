# Day two: Brindle's commission, and the mischief reactions shared by every job.

== day2_morning
* Morning on Quietwater. The drum gardens hiss with misters and somebody is frying moon-plum fritters.
* Your tablet has new mail.
end

== brindle_arrives
* A boxy yellow tug bumps gently into the bay door, backs up, and tries again. On the second go it fits.
brindle happy: Good morning, {name}! I have come as a customer. I have never been a customer before. I am very excited.
brindle thoughtful: I have timed the Bucket in races. In corners it feels like... like a shopping trolley with one bad wheel.
brindle sad: My corner times are my worst times. Worse than my straight-line times. Worse than my queueing times, and I am very good at queueing.
> What would make it better? => better
> Shopping trolleys can be fun. => trolley
-- trolley
brindle laughing: That is true! I once rode one down the cargo ramp. It was the second-best day of my life.
=> better
-- better
brindle: I would like the Bucket to turn like a spinning top, and stick to corners like a sticker on a lunchbox.
brindle: I have ninety pebs. I also have a Hushdrive I found in a salvage bin. It is in excellent condition. It hums like a sleepy cat.
brindle thoughtful: I do not need it. My Bucket is supposed to clank. Clanking is part of its personality.
brindle happy: Also, I should tell you something. If you make the Bucket better, I will use it to beat you in races. In a friendly way.
> Deal, rival. => deal
> I'll make it a little bit better. => deal
-- deal
brindle happy: Hooray. I will go and watch the misters in the drum gardens. They make small rainbows.
! job brindle_1
end

== brindle_1_delighted
* Brindle climbs in, takes the Bucket once around the bay, and comes back very slowly, as if it doesn't want the turn to end.
brindle laughing: {name}! The corners! They are fun now! I turned three times on purpose!
brindle happy: My corner time has gone down by an amount I do not have a unit for. I am going to invent one. I will call it a {name}.
brindle: Here are your pebs, and the Hushdrive. Please give it a good home.
end

== brindle_1_pleased
brindle happy: It turns better! Not quite like a spinning top. More like a spinning plate. That is still very good.
brindle: Here are your pebs, and the Hushdrive, as promised.
end

== brindle_1_lukewarm
brindle thoughtful: Hm. It is mostly the same trolley. But it is a cleaner trolley. Thank you for trying.
brindle: The Hushdrive is still yours. A deal is a deal.
end

== brindle_1_prank
brindle surprised: Oh! There is something new.
brindle laughing: I love it. Is it a gift? It is the funniest thing I have ever owned. I am keeping it forever.
* Somehow, that makes you feel worse than if it had been angry.
end

== brindle_1_sabotage
brindle thoughtful: Something feels... wrong. I do not have a word for it.
brindle sad: I trusted the readout. I trust most readouts. Maybe I should not.
* Brindle's antenna droops. It pays in full anyway.
end

== odessa_1_prank

odessa laughing: The grandkids are going to lose their minds. I'm never taking it off.
end

== odessa_1_sabotage
odessa sad: I don't know what you did, love, but she's not right. I can feel it in the stick.
odessa thoughtful: I'm sure you had your reasons. I'll have her looked at somewhere else, I think.
end

== mischief_prank
* They notice the prank. There's a long pause, and then a reluctant snort of laughter.
end
