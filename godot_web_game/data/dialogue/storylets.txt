# Storylets: short arcs that attach to a walk-in (see data/storylets.json). "guest" is them.
# "! story flag" marks this story; "? flag => label" reads its flags and the game's. The handoff
# sets tier_delighted, tier_pleased or tier_lukewarm before its beat plays.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the heirloom

== heirloom_found
* As {guest} heads off, something rattles loose from a cabin vent: a small tin, dented and very old.
* It's warm, as if someone held it a long time.
> Keep it safe to give back. => safe
> Peek inside. => peek
-- peek
* Inside: a tiny brass compass, a pressed flower, and a photo of someone laughing in a ship very like the {guest_ship}.
! story peeked
* You close the lid carefully.
end
-- safe
* You set it on the shelf by the tea nook, where you won't forget it.
end

== heirloom_return
you: Before you go. This fell out of a vent.
guest surprised: Oh! Oh, I thought I'd lost it for good.
? peeked => peeked
guest happy: It was my grandmother's. She flew the {guest_ship} before me. Thank you for keeping it safe.
! story kept_secret
end
-- peeked
guest thoughtful: Did you look inside?
> I did. I'm sorry. => honest
> No. => lie
-- honest
guest happy: That's all right. It's nice that someone else has seen her laugh.
! story honest
end
-- lie
guest thoughtful: Hm. The flower's the wrong way up.
guest: Never mind. Thank you for giving it back.
end

== heirloom_thanks
* {guest} drops by, holding a paper bag that smells of toasted seeds.
? honest => warm
? kept_secret => warm
guest: I wanted to say thank you properly. For the tin.
* They leave the bag on the counter and go before you can say anything.
! give cake_seed 1
end
-- warm
guest happy: My grandmother's recipe. She'd have liked you.
guest laughing: She'd have told you all about the time she flew the {guest_ship} through a comet tail. Twice.
! give cake_seed 2
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the rival shop

== rival_quote
guest thoughtful: I should mention. Gleam and Company across the ring quoted me half your price.
guest: Promised her faster, too. Shinier. They have a lot of chrome over there.
> Then why come here? => why
> We'll see who's right. => see
-- why
guest grumpy: Because their chrome fell off my friend's ship in a light breeze.
! story challenged
end
-- see
guest laughing: We will. I'll be comparing.
! story challenged
end

== rival_verdict
? tier_delighted => won
? tier_lukewarm => lost
guest thoughtful: Not bad. Not Gleam, but not bad. I suppose Gleam isn't Gleam either.
end
-- won
guest surprised: All right. I'll say it. Gleam couldn't have done this.
guest happy: I'm telling everyone. Loudly. Near their shop.
! story won
end
-- lost
guest grumpy: I'll be honest, Gleam's looking better from here.
! story lost
end

== rival_news
? won => won
* The dock bulletin has a new notice up: GLEAM & CO. NOW OFFERING DISCOUNTS. INQUIRE WITHIN. PLEASE.
* Someone has drawn a little rocket ship beside it, heading for your shop.
end
-- won
* {guest} stops by the bay door, grinning.
guest laughing: Gleam's put a sign up. Discounts. Apparently business is slow.
guest happy: I may have had something to do with that. You're welcome.
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- courting

== courting_ask
guest thoughtful: Can I tell you something? It's a bit silly.
> Of course. => tell
-- tell
guest sad: There's someone at the drum gardens. They fly the prettiest little skiff. I want to ask them to the lantern night.
guest thoughtful: And I thought, if the {guest_ship} looked her best, I might be brave enough.
> She'll look wonderful. => kind
> You'll be brave either way. => brave
-- kind
guest happy: Thank you. Really.
end
-- brave
guest surprised: Oh. Do you think so?
guest happy: I'll hold on to that.
! story encouraged
end

== courting_luck
? tier_lukewarm => nervous
guest happy: She looks wonderful. I feel taller just standing next to her.
guest laughing: Wish me luck!
end
-- nervous
guest thoughtful: Well. It's still me asking, not the ship. Wish me luck anyway?
end

== courting_report
* {guest} comes in at a run.
? tier_lukewarm => mixed
? encouraged => yes
guest happy: They said yes! They said the {guest_ship} was charming. And then they said I was charming. In that order, but still.
! give tea_drift_bloom 1
end
-- yes
guest laughing: They said yes! And I asked before I even showed them the ship. I was brave, like you said.
guest happy: Here. For the advice. Their favorite tea, now mine too.
! give tea_drift_bloom 2
end
-- mixed
guest thoughtful: They said "maybe". Maybe's not no.
guest happy: We're getting tea next week. Maybe's a start.
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the stowaway

== stowaway_found
* While you're giving the {guest_ship} a once-over, something flutters in the intake: a space-moth, silver and blinking, with three eggs tucked in the fluff.
> Coax them out gently. => gentle
> Leave them be for now. => leave
-- gentle
* It takes a saucer of warm tea and a lot of patience, but the moth and its eggs settle into a teacup on the shelf.
! story rehomed
end
-- leave
* You work around the nest. The moth watches you the whole time.
! story nest_left
end

== stowaway_handoff
? rehomed => rehomed
guest surprised: Is that... is something humming in the intake?
* The moth pokes its head out and blinks at {guest}.
guest laughing: Well! I suppose I've got a co-pilot.
end
-- rehomed
* As {guest} climbs in, the moth flutters off the shelf, circles the {guest_ship} once and comes back to its teacup.
guest happy: Was that living in my ship? And you moved it somewhere safe? That's kind.
end

== stowaway_visit
* There's a tapping at the window by the tea nook.
? rehomed => hatched
* It's the space-moth from the {guest_ship}, with {guest} waving behind it.
guest happy: She wouldn't stop fluttering at your door. I think she wanted to say hello.
end
-- hatched
* The teacup on the shelf is empty except for three tiny silver shells. Outside the window, four space-moths are circling.
* One of them lands on the rim of the teacup and leaves something there: a single moon-plum, perfectly ripe.
! give cake_moon_plum 1
end
